Comprehensive Assessment Framework and Item Bank: Class 8 English (Poorvi)The alignment of middle school language education with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and the National Curriculum Framework (NCF) 2023 necessitates a transition from rote memorization to competency-based evaluation. The newly introduced Class 8 English textbook, Poorvi, serves as a multidimensional pedagogical tool designed to foster linguistic proficiency, moral reasoning, and critical thinking. For students in government schools, assessments must be linguistically accessible while progressively challenging their cognitive faculties. The following assessment framework provides a calibrated item bank containing exactly 30 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) for all fifteen chapters across the five core units. Each chapter's item bank is meticulously stratified into ten easy questions targeting factual recall, ten medium questions focusing on inferential comprehension, and ten hard questions demanding thematic synthesis, vocabulary decoding, and literary analysis.Unit 1: Wit and WisdomThe inaugural unit establishes the foundational theme that intellect and sharp reasoning often triumph over brute force and superficial appearances. Through historical fiction, humorous poetry, and dramatic prose, students are introduced to protagonists who utilize emotional intelligence and keen observation to navigate complex social dilemmas. The pedagogical objective here is to develop students' deductive reasoning and their ability to detect literary devices such as irony and metaphor. In the first chapter, Tenali Rama’s diplomatic resolution of a royal marital dispute demonstrates the power of analogical reasoning over direct confrontation. The subsequent poem introduces a satirical critique of extreme artificial order at the expense of natural beauty, utilizing puns and hyperbole to challenge conventional aesthetics. Finally, the third chapter highlights the distinction between mere knowledge and applied wisdom, as four young travelers utilize forensic-style observation to prove their innocence to a king. The assessment items below are designed to measure students' grasp of these nuanced themes, ensuring a gradual progression from basic plot comprehension to advanced thematic analysis.Chapter 1: The
