# Teacher Sathi — Official Brand Guidelines & Voice Standards

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
**Teacher Sathi** is **India's Dedicated NCERT AI Co-Pilot & Teaching Assistant**, engineered specifically for Indian educators teaching in CBSE, Kendriya Vidyalaya (KVS), Jawahar Navodaya (JNV), and State Board classrooms.

- **Primary Value Proposition**: "Saves Educators 12 Hours Weekly on NCERT Planning."
- **Brand Mascot / Companion**: **Sathi Genie (साथी)** — An approachable, intelligent classroom companion supporting teachers on 75" interactive smart displays.

---

## 2. Palette & Design Tokens
- **Primary Brand Green**: `#14532D` (Forest Green - Trust & Growth)
- **Secondary Emerald**: `#15803D` / `#0F5B38` (Vibrant Accent)
- **Gold / Accent**: `#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24` (Energy & Excellence)
- **Classroom High-Contrast Dark**: `#1A2E20` (Optimized for 75" High-Luminance Projectors)
- **Clean Background**: `#F8FAFC` / `#E8F3EA`

## 3. Typography Standards
- **English Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Extrabold, Bold, 8pt Grid spacing)
- **Hindi & Regional Body**: `Mukta` / `Noto Sans Devanagari` (Readable, clear rendering on low-res screens)

---

## 4. Compliance & Trust Seals
- **NCERT & NEP 2020 Aligned**: Indexed directly to Class 6 to 10 textbooks.
- **DPDP Act 2023 Compliant**: 256-bit encrypted data protection.
- **75" Smartboard Native**: Zero-delay QR session handshakes.
