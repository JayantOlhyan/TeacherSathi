## 1. Vibe and References
- **Vibe in four words**: Tactile, pedagogical, crisp, empowering.
- **Medium references**:
  - *Hardware*: 75-inch Interactive Flat Panel (IFP) Smartboard running an intentional, high-contrast classroom OS.
  - *Print*: NCERT official textbook typography with crisp bilingual Hindi/English typesetting.
  - *Digital*: High-performance live engineering tools (e.g. Linear, Figma workspace) adapted for rural & urban Indian classrooms with low cognitive load.

---

## 2. The Scroll Journey (Beats)

1. **Beat 1: The Wake (Surface Hero)**
   - The visitor arrives into an illuminated modern smart classroom. The 75" interactive smartboard is already active with NCERT Class 8 Science.
2. **Beat 2: The Honest Tension**
   - The reality of Indian teaching: 14+ hours lost every week to manual lesson planning, slide formatting, translation, and test paper typing.
3. **Beat 3: The Metamorphic Core (The Engineered Peak)**
   - One NCERT chapter transforms through scrolling into all six pedagogical artifacts: bilingual slides, explainer video, conceptual mind map, live student quiz, CBSE question bank, and a print-ready PDF worksheet.
4. **Beat 4: The Tactile Classroom Console**
   - Direct interactive agency: immediate bilingual toggle (Hindi & English), live slide navigation, and real NCERT chapter exploration across subjects.
5. **Beat 5: The Empirical Time-Saved Ledger**
   - Concrete, transparent arithmetic of hours saved per week by board type and class load (CBSE, KVS, State Boards).
6. **Beat 6: The Free Teacher Commitment**
   - Unpressured resolution: 100% free forever for individual teachers with one-click lesson generation.

## 5. Signature Move
**The Dual-Mode Smartboard Morphic Console**:
A bespoke, zero-jank GPU-accelerated scrubber and tactile switchboard that lets teachers scrub through all 6 pedagogical formats (Slides, Video, Mind Map, Quiz, Question Bank, Print Worksheet) while maintaining bilingual state synchronization (Hindi & English) and instant preview scaling.
