Unit 3: Mystery and MagicThe third unit transitions from realism to narratives steeped in deductive logic, fantastical imagination, and the awe-inspiring phenomena of the natural world. These texts are curated to stimulate curiosity and sharpen critical thinking. "The Case of the Fifth Word" operates as a classic whodunit, requiring students to follow Encyclopedia Brown’s lateral thinking to decode a complex linguistic cipher based on omitted letters and anagrams. Following this exercise in pure logic, "The Magic Brush of Dreams" introduces a folkloric poem addressing class conflict and the ethical use of power, where creativity serves as a weapon against greed. The unit culminates with a non-fiction exploration, "Spectacular Wonders," highlighting India's geographic marvels, such as bioluminescent bays and meteorite craters, effectively bridging the gap between scientific reality and perceived magic. The corresponding assessments test the students' ability to track logical progressions, interpret allegorical symbolism, and differentiate between man-made and natural environmental phenomena.Chapter
